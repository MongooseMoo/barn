## Realistic bench_differ corpus (issue #276, plan B6): verb-call, error-idiom and lookup shaped work.
## Format: `name: moo statements` per line; run with `python scripts/bench_differ.py --corpus experiments/corpus-real.txt`.
## Fixture assumptions (Test.db from moo-conformance-tests): #0 has $nothing/$object/$server_options/$waif/$anonymous
## and verbs do_login_command/handle_*; #1 Root Class has no props or verbs; #3 is the wizard the harness evals as.
## Every workload creates its own objects/verbs and recycles them (try/finally where it touches #0 or $server_options),
## so the fixture is unchanged for the next line. Return values avoid object numbers so both engines agree.
## Recursion depth is 40, not 50: Toast caps the activation stack at 50 and the harness already spends two frames
## (the emergency-mode line plus the eval() wrapper). Property names avoid Toast built-ins (name/owner/r/w/f/a/...).
