# One implementation per store operation (Wave 3c): measurements

Branch refactor/no-twins on 4449b485, measured at 17edcafa against a detached worktree of
4449b485 carrying the same db/store/direct_txn_bench_test.go (six direct-transaction
benchmarks over the 6-deep, 8-verbs-per-object chain of benchChainStore). Allocation counts
do not depend on machine load. ns/op does; it is shown, both trees were run back to back on a
loaded machine (62% at the start), and it is not a gate.

Command, run on each tree:

```
go test ./db/store -run '^$' -bench 'BenchmarkDirect' -benchmem -count=3
```

All three runs of each benchmark gave the same allocs/op and B/op.

| Benchmark | allocs/op before | after | B/op before | after | ns/op before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DirectFindCallableVerb | 5 | 12 | 40 | 600 | 1105-1129 | 1344-1467 |
| DirectFindProperty | 5 | 12 | 40 | 1632 | 561-568 | 2646-2949 |
| DirectContents | 1 | 1 | 48 | 48 | 99-107 | 76-85 |
| DirectSetPropertyValue | 24 | 59 | 2936 | 7976 | 4842-5069 | 9906-10579 |
| DirectSetObjectName | 25 | 59 | 3000 | 8128 | 4788-5330 | 9566-11164 |
| DirectMoveObject | 84 | 176 | 9736 | 22351 | 11746-14315 | 23023-25716 |

Where the read allocations come from (`-memprofile`, alloc_space, FindProperty and
FindCallableVerb benchmarks): walkProperty 48%, walkVerb 51%, objIDSet.add 14% (cumulative
inside the walks). That is the private scratch the design gives the shared direct txn
(design 3.2 item 4): a fresh propScratch/verbScratch per walk whose queue, visited list and
step record grow from empty. The step record exists only for the per-transaction memo, which
is off for the direct txn.

The writes allocate the private writer (StoreTxn and its objects map), the staged write maps,
writesByObject's map and objectWrites, and a private copy of each written object at staging
plus the published clone at publication, where the old twin cloned once.

Per server task, the direct reads on its path are two FindProperty calls
(builtins/limits.go:76-78, GetMaxStackDepth from engine/task_factory.go:29) and one to three
FindVerb calls (engine/task_factory.go:101, :171, :270; engine/call_verb.go:280): about 14 to
28 more allocations and, at the ns/op above, a few microseconds per task. Direct writes run
on coarse paths only (builtins/objects.go:254, :581; builtins/objects_movement.go:161;
server/input_processor.go:839).
